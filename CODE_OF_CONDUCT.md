# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

- Be respectful and constructive. Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and accept differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code, and may restrict participation of anyone who repeatedly violates it.

Report unacceptable behavior to **contact@doganddev.eu**. Reports are handled confidentially.
